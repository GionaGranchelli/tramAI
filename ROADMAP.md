TramAI's roadmap authority is intentionally split by purpose:

1. [`docs/LONG-TERM-ROADMAP-0.7-0.10.md`](docs/LONG-TERM-ROADMAP-0.7-0.10.md) owns the cross-release product sequence.
2. Release-specific roadmap files under `docs/` own the authoritative scope for each release.
3. [`docs/design/`](docs/design/) contains supporting architecture and design material; design notes do not expand a release unless the release roadmap explicitly adopts that work.
