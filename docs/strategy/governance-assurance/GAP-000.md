# GAP-000 — Governance Assurance Strategic Reset

> Decision: **Strategic direction approved, 2026-10-11**. Implementation and detailed regulatory mappings remain subject to normal engineering review.
> Scope: docs-only product and roadmap direction; **no regulatory conformity claim**.

## Decision

Governance assurance becomes TramAI's primary cross-release strategic priority. Preserve the product identity as a Kotlin-first, JVM-native *governed AI runtime*, now explicitly supporting the lifecycle: define governance intent, enforce it, verify control behavior, attribute evidence to immutable revisions, and map those proofs to requirements applicable to a particular AI system and operator.

**Do not** become a generic ISO management-system product, create a second policy engine, claim EU AI Act conformity or ISO/IEC 42001 certification, or impose a hosted control plane on embedded deployments.

## Requirements

1. **Applicability before control coverage.** Evaluate jurisdiction, intended purpose, operator role, system category and law/standard version before mapping any purported obligations.
2. **Authoritative runtime enforcement.** Preserve existing classification-before-exposure, restrictive composition, approval and final-cancellation semantics.
3. **Evidence truthfulness.** Coverage states differentiate verified technical control, partial support, externally evidenced organizational responsibility, unknown, and not applicable; a successful log hash does not prove the source fact is true.
4. **Governed change.** Versioned, typed, authorized configuration lifecycle with controlled activation and immutable history. Distinguish live, governed, structural and emergency restrictive controls.
5. **One engine, many clients.** Dashboard, GitOps, configuration DSL, simulator, auditor reports and REST APIs are authoring or read projections of canonical authority.
6. **Security and privacy.** No unrestricted raw prompt/PII retention for audit or learning. No policy or approval widening through stale snapshots.
7. **Embedded first.** A standalone JVM application continues to run without centralized SaaS, with optional enterprise APIs and persistence.

## Legal and standard boundaries

EU AI Act: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, consolidated version dated 2026-07-27 (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng). Operator duties are role-dependent and dates may have transitional regimes. Legal applicability is not determinable solely from a library setting.

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html) concerns an organization's AI management system. Detailed clause/Annex A coverage needs qualified review against the licensed normative text. TramAI may supply technical controls, change records and evidence references, not organizational certification.

## Approved sequencing and non-goals

- Keep the current 0.7 release cut authoritative; ongoing P0 correctness/security work continues.
- Start GAP-100 applicability/control-to-evidence inventory and CFG-0001 configuration authority as architecture and test-contract work.
- Rebaseline 0.8–0.10 explicitly; preserve earlier features when dependencies and governance outcomes justify them.
- Product positioning and public claims may be revised separately only after implementation/proofs warrant it.
- No runtime implementation, complete compliance audit, new legal claims or automatic ISO certification in GAP-000.
