## C1.1 Source-of-truth and producer inventory

Read from source at this SHA. "Kind" is what the source *is*: live authoritative state, a recorded
authorization decision, a producer declaration, telemetry, or nothing at all.

| Domain | Owner / source | Kind | Identity & correlation available | Version / ordering | Read currency | What a consumer may not infer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Two findings worth stating plainly: the policy/tool families are **caller-driven** rather than
engine-emitted, and `provider.route` has **no producer at all**. Both are frozen facts of 0.7.4b, not
assumptions — and both are why the decision side of this seam is port-driven today.

## C1.2 The projection contract

Small, typed, read-only. No metadata map, no generic provenance ontology, no extension mechanism.

- `FactAuthority` — `AUTHORITATIVE_PERSISTED`, `POLICY_AUTHORIZATION_RECORD`, `PRODUCER_DECLARED`,
  `TELEMETRY`, `NOT_PRODUCED`.
- `FactFreshness` — `CURRENT_AT_VERSION`, `HISTORICAL`, `EVENTUAL_KNOWN_LAG`, `STALE_OR_UNKNOWN`,
  `ABSENT`, with `from(consistency, watermark)` mapping the **existing** `QueryConsistency`:
  `AUTHORITATIVE` → current, `PROJECTION` + watermark → known lag, `PROJECTION` without watermark →
  unknown. The established read classification is reused, not duplicated.
- `ProjectedFact<T>(value, authority, freshness, established, mustNotInfer, observedVersion,
  sourceVersion, watermark)` — one fact, its authority, its temporal standing, what it established,
  and what a consumer must not infer.
- `DecisionProjection(kind, latest, completeness, duplicatesIgnored, staleIgnored)`,
  `GovernanceProjection(registration, declaredRunIdentity, decisions, telemetry)`,
  `RecordedDecision`, `TelemetryObservation`, `DeclaredRunIdentity`, `DeclaredBoundary`,
  `GovernanceDecisionFactSource` (read port), `GovernanceProjectionReader` (the seam).

**Enforced invariants** (constructed facts that violate them cannot exist):

1. a fact exists exactly when its freshness is not `ABSENT`;
2. only `NOT_PRODUCED` or the owning authority may report absence;
3. `NOT_PRODUCED` cannot carry a fresh fact;
4. `CURRENT_AT_VERSION` is reserved for `AUTHORITATIVE_PERSISTED` and requires a version;
5. `HISTORICAL` requires a source version or an observed version;
6. `EVENTUAL_KNOWN_LAG` requires a watermark — unknown lag is never zero;
7. `PRODUCER_DECLARED` and `POLICY_AUTHORIZATION_RECORD` facts must state what may not be inferred;
8. `COMPLETE` requires the source's own positions;
9. one entry per decision kind, and **routing records are source-declared at best** — no engine
   producer exists;
10. a recorded policy/tool decision is **never** `AUTHORITATIVE_PERSISTED`.

Invariants 9 and 10 are the upgrade guards the task demands; the mutation killed below shows they
bite at construction, not only in assertions.

## C1.3 Frozen v1 limitations, carried into reads

| Limitation | How the projection carries it | Discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| Identity is **declared**, not authenticated (T1 open) | declared identity is `PRODUCER_DECLARED` + `STALE_OR_UNKNOWN`, never authoritative, and its `mustNotInfer` names T1 every time | `a complete declared identity stays declared, never authoritative` |
| Boundary provenance proves strength, not origin (G2) | the boundary-origin limitation travels **with any fact that names a boundary** | `a registered boundary named by a record grants no verified-origin claim` |
| `ALLOW` proves authorization, not completion (G3) | facts are `POLICY_AUTHORIZATION_RECORD`; the record type has no completion/result/payload field at all | `an ALLOW record is authorization evidence and the read surface cannot carry completion` |
| `provider.route` has no live producer (G4) | `NOT_PRODUCED` + `ABSENT`, never "no routing happened" | `a missing provider route fact is unsupported, never an assertion that no routing happened` |
| Current state must not replace missing historical facts | decision facts are `HISTORICAL` at their own source position and are never stamped with the current registration version | `a historical decision is never restated at the current registration version` |
| Telemetry must not become authority (P0.8) | telemetry is read through a separate method into its own slot and cannot enter a decision projection | `telemetry reporting a selected route cannot become routing evidence` |

## C1.4 The read seam

Data flow: `WorkloadControlPlaneQueries.authoritative(scope)` → registration fact (authoritative,
current at the observed version) ; `GovernanceDecisionFactSource.decisionsFor(runId)` → folded per
kind ; `telemetryFor(runId)` → separate telemetry fact ; all wrapped in `ProjectedFact` with
authority from one classification table.

Authority is **assigned by the projection, never claimed by the source**: a source returning routing
records still produces source-declared routing facts.

Real versus scripted, stated without blur: the registration half runs against the **real**
`WorkloadRegistrationAuthority` over `InMemoryWorkloadRegistrationStore` in every test (real
versions, real compare-and-set, real absence). The decision/telemetry half is **driven by a scripted
source** — because no in-repo production reader or producer exists for those families, which is the
frozen state of G1/G4, not a shortcut. Wiring a production decision reader (audit store / evidence
bundle loader) is the next slice.

## C1.5 Projection authority stays read-only

- Structural: `GovernanceProjectionReader`'s constructor takes exactly `WorkloadControlPlaneQueries`
  and `GovernanceDecisionFactSource` — asserted by reflection, so adding a command port, a store or an
  engine to this seam fails the build.
- Structural: no type reachable from the reader is `WorkloadControlPlaneCommands`, `RegisterOutcome`,
  `MetadataUpdateOutcome`, `LifecycleTransitionOutcome`, `RegisteredWorkload` or
  `ConfigurationFingerprint` — the projection cannot authorize, mutate registration, change
  enforcement strength or read the mutation witness.
- Behavioural: reading twice returns an equal projection and leaves the store's `stateVersion`
  unchanged, with exactly one read per source and no effect invoked
  (`reading the projection changes no authoritative state and invokes no effect`).
- A command still goes to `WorkloadControlPlaneCommands`; projected state is never an authorization
  input. The projection exposes no method that returns an authorization.

## C1.6 Ordering, duplication and freshness

| Rule | Implementation | Discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate source records do not duplicate the fact | identical records are folded; the count is reported | `a duplicated source event does not duplicate the projected fact` |
| An older record cannot overwrite a newer state | the highest source position wins regardless of delivery order; superseded positions are counted | `an older record cannot overwrite the newer position` |
| A missing intermediate event is not completeness | contiguity is checked against the source's own positions | `a fold with a missing intermediate event does not claim completeness` |
| Unknown lag is unknown, not zero | no ordering basis ⇒ `UNKNOWN` completeness and `STALE_OR_UNKNOWN` freshness | `freshness is not invented when the source reports no watermark` |
| A stale projection is not a fresh authorization witness | the seam has no authorization path at all; freshness never exceeds the source | C1.5 discriminators |
| Rebuild/replay executes no external effect | the seam only reads; asserted by the idempotence test | `reading the projection changes no authoritative state and invokes no effect` |

The existing mechanisms this respects, rather than replaces: `WorkloadStateVersion` monotonicity with
compare-and-set on the authority, and the audit store's per-stream `sequence_number` with a hash
chain and a stream head. No new persistence engine is introduced.

## Review findings and corrections

Both findings were accepted as correct. Both change the contract itself, not only a future adapter.

### Finding A — completeness could be overstated

`completenessOf` derived completeness from the observed positions alone — so a single record at position
100 reported `COMPLETE`, when positions before and after it may exist — and the fold filtered records by
family before assessing contiguity, while audit positions belong to the stream, so another family's
position read as a hole in this one's history.

Now: `Completeness.COMPLETE` requires a range the source **attests** it read
(`DecisionFactRead.assertedRange`); `DecisionProjection` refuses the claim when that range is absent
(`COMPLETE ⟹ coverage != null`); the fold carries the read's stream-level assessment instead of deriving
one per family; and an unattested read reports `UNKNOWN`, never `COMPLETE`. An empty read over an attested
range is self-inconsistent — the projection reports `UNKNOWN` rather than inventing a specific hole or
claiming coverage. `GAPS_PRESENT` now means what it says: positions inside an attested range that this
read did not observe.

### Finding B — a missing record could imply a nonexistent producer

An empty read was graded `NOT_PRODUCED`. It now reports `NOT_OBSERVED`: the read found nothing, which is
not the same statement as nothing ever being produced — emission of these records is optional in this
repository and coverage may be partial. `NOT_PRODUCED` is reserved for a family whose missing producer is
itself established, which is the frozen G4 position for `provider.route`. A caller-supplied declaration
that was never supplied is `NOT_PRODUCED` rather than `NOT_OBSERVED`, because no source read was
involved in its absence.

### Finding C — the freshness classification was inverted (blocking)

`GovernanceProjectionReader.registrationFact` bound its branch to
`read.consistency == QueryConsistency.AUTHORITATIVE` but named it `projected`, so an **authoritative**
read was classified `EVENTUAL_KNOWN_LAG` and a **projection** read `CURRENT_AT_VERSION` — the second
claiming an authority the source never had, in the file this task exists to keep honest. The watermark
was inverted with it. The invariant that should have caught it did not, because the reader labelled
every registration read `AUTHORITATIVE_PERSISTED` regardless of consistency, so `CURRENT_AT_VERSION`
still satisfied its guard.

The mapping now lives in one place, `GovernanceFactClassification.freshnessFor(consistency, watermark)`:
`AUTHORITATIVE -> CURRENT_AT_VERSION`; `PROJECTION` with a named watermark -> `EVENTUAL_KNOWN_LAG`;
`PROJECTION` without one -> `STALE_OR_UNKNOWN`. The reader passes the watermark it can actually name,
so the third row is reachable by contract rather than dead code. Discriminators: one against the **real**
registration authority asserting `CURRENT_AT_VERSION` with no watermark, one through a port double that
reports a `PROJECTION` read and asserts it can never become current, and one on the table itself for the
no-watermark row.

### Finding D — cross-run record attribution (contract integrity)

The reader asked `decisionsFor(workflowRunId)` and projected whatever came back, so a source returning
records for run B contributed facts to run A's projection. It now fails the read **closed**: a record
whose `workflowRunId` is not the requested run raises rather than being projected. Dropping such records
silently would have been worse — the projection could then claim completeness over data it had quietly
discarded, which is the fail-open shape this task is meant to remove. This is consistency between the
request and the response, not authentication (T1 remains unresolved). Discriminator:
`a decision record for another run is refused, not projected`.

### Discriminators added

- `a single observed position is not completeness without an attested source range`
- `completeness is a stream property, so another family position is not a gap`
- `completeness cannot be claimed without the attested range it rests on`
- `an empty read is not evidence that nothing was produced` (asserts the `NOT_OBSERVED` versus
  `NOT_PRODUCED` split in one read)
- `a live authoritative registration read is current at its own version`
- `a projected registration read is never labelled current`
- `a projection that cannot name its watermark is not claimed current`
- `a decision record for another run is refused, not projected`

## Unsupported and unresolved

- **Not established**: a production decision-fact reader (`tool.permission`, `policy.decision`), a
  `provider.route` producer, authenticated workload origin (T1), event-origin attribution (G2), any
  execution-completion attestation, materialised storage, refresh policy, watermarking, retention,
  or any public/HTTP query surface.
- **Not claimed**: that the frozen schema gained authority (it did not — 0.7.4b stands unchanged),
  that telemetry became evidence, or that the strength vocabulary's unused values are exercised.
- **Classification (owner)**: **CONTRACT QUALIFIED — IMPLEMENTATION DEFERRED**. The registration path
  is verified end to end over the real authority; the decision path is verified through a port with
  **no production implementation in-repo**. The production decision source is the next goal, not a
  reason to widen this one.
- **Withdrawn**: the earlier claim of `PASS — AUTHORITY-AWARE READ BOUNDARY VERIFIED` is withdrawn. It
rested on a completeness rule and an absence vocabulary that two review findings showed were stronger
than the sources could support — and, as two further findings showed, on a freshness mapping that was
inverted and a record-attribution gap that let one run's records be projected as another's. All four
are corrected above with discriminators; the classification is the owner's.

## Next implementation boundary

1. Implement `GovernanceDecisionFactSource` over the real sources: the audit store for
   `policy.decision` / `tool.permission`, and the evidence bundle loader for imported records — using
   the source's own `sequence_number` as `sourceSequence` so gap detection is real, not simulated.
   The adapter's first deliverable is not the records but a trustworthy **range attestation**: what the
   source covered, and how it knows. Without it the projection can only report `UNKNOWN` completeness,
   which is the correct outcome and the reason the attestation has to come from the source rather than
   be inferred from the records it returned.
2. Decide the projection's public shape (server read API) with an API-compatibility review, including
   whether it stays internal until the timeline work needs it (P0.8).
3. Materialised storage, refresh and watermarking belong to Wave C items 3–4, deliberately not
   started here.
